## What the HOC analysis actually found
- **H1 2026 net revenue USD 832.5m** (consolidated), USD 696.3m attributable, up ~60% YoY.
- **The v2 estimate was 12.2% light.** Valuing output as gold-equivalent ounces x the gold
  price understated by USD 91.7m: HOC converts silver at 77:1 (reset annually - it was 83:1
  in 2025) but silver realised at 53.6:1. Build revenue metal by metal. C13 marked SUPERSEDED.
- **The method was back-tested** against H1 2025, reproducing HOC's reported gross revenue
  to within 0.05%. That is what makes it defensible to a member.
- **The hedge.** Gold realised at 88.8% of the market average whilst silver realised at
  99.1%. Explanation was in the derivatives note of the PRIOR interim accounts: a June 2023
  Citibank deal, 50,000 oz/yr at USD 2,167 against Mara Rosa. Reconciles to 0.4%. It cost
  ~USD 70.9m of H1 revenue and rolls off after the 2027 leg - a structural tailwind.
- **FY2026 read-across: USD 1,589m vs consensus 1,660m (-4.3%)** - because H1 was the
  stronger half on PRICE, not because the mines are underperforming. Puts the shares on
  ~9.8x forward rather than 8.7x.
- **The recompute pass caught 4 errors in 70 checks** before issue - including the hedge
  cost labelled "a year" when it was a half-year figure (44% understatement of the annual
  impact), and two inconsistent readings of the AISC overrun in the same report. Forecast
  PBT moved 640 -> 624 as a result.
