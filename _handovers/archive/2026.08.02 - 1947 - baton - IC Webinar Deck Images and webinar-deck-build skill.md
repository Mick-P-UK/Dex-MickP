  portfolio width, pair centred vertically between 0.55in and 7.30in, portfolio width
  capped at 11.0in.

  edit only the new one, so the previous version survives as a fallback.
- The new skill is TWO commands, not one pass. Mick may start a deck on the 29th when
  the month-end images do not yet exist, and refresh-images gets re-run whenever a
  single image is corrected after the fact.
- Date rolling touches only known slots (master header, title, diary, captions) and
  REPORTS every other date. The Mick's Radar pages carry "Mick's View at <date>" and
  must stay fixed.
- The skill never deletes content. Stale slides are reported for Mick to decide.
