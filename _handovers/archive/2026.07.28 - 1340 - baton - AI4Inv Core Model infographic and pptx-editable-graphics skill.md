## Key technical findings (do not rediscover these)

- Mick's decks carry the STOCK Office 2007 colour scheme in
  ppt/theme/theme1.xml (4F81BD / C0504D / 9BBB59). The real branding is in
  literal srgbClr values on the slides. ALWAYS read the slide census.
- The .ai Webinar pack is a dark navy frame around a LIGHT content panel
  (EDF1F2). Mick's light-theme preference holds inside the dark frame. Do not
  build dark-background graphics for it.
- python-pptx writes a <p:style> block whose effectRef idx="2" re-injects a drop
  shadow even after shadow.inherit = False. Zero lnRef, fillRef and effectRef.
- Block Arc adj1/adj2 are 60000ths of a degree, clockwise from three o'clock.
  Donut adj3 is a fraction of shape WIDTH, not radius.
- Curved text cannot be both produced by python-pptx and editable. Rotate normal
  text boxes to the tangent instead.
