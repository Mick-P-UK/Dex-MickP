## Thread character
Short design thread. Mick uploaded the "AI for Investing 29th July 2026" title-slide
thumbnail and asked for a diagonal bright green CTA reading "Click HERE for PowerPoint PDF."
It grew into a house-style decision and a skill version bump.

## Decisions made
- Plain bright green text across a dark blue slide read as a WATERMARK, not a link.
  Mick proposed putting the text on a narrow angled rectangular shape. He was right -
  the band makes it read as a button.
- Two banded variants were built and compared: light grey band and charcoal band.
  Cedric recommended charcoal on contrast grounds; MICK CHOSE LIGHT GREY and
  downloaded it. Light grey is now the standing option.
- HOUSE CTA PRESET (confirmed): light grey band (224,224,224) at alpha 240, mid-grey
  3px border, bright green text (0,255,0) with a MANDATORY 3px dark outline (20,20,20),
  soft black drop shadow behind the band (offset +6, Gaussian blur 7), corner-to-corner
  angle from atan2(height, width), 64px font on a 1366x769 thumbnail, band padding 48x26.
- Scope of the preset: diy-investors.ai MONTHLY WEBINAR thumbnails, and any slide with
  a dark blue / dark tech background (the .ai Webinar template). Plain red text remains
  the fallback for everything else.
- The 3px dark text outline is not decoration - bright green on light grey is low
  contrast and the letters mush without it. Do not drop it.
- Charcoal band retained in the skill as an explicit override only.
