## Core thesis
- Transformed from junior explorer to a major Australian gold and copper producer after acquiring 100% of the Telfer mine and Havieron project from Newmont (completed 4 December 2024).
- Regional hub-and-spoke model anchored on the Telfer mill - the only operating mill in WA's Paterson Province - with 20Mtpa processing capacity across two trains.
- Controls ~15Moz gold and ~645kt copper in total group mineral resources.
- 100% unhedged upside to spot gold, with AUD gold put options setting a downside floor.

## Key assets (all 100% owned)
- Telfer (gold-copper): active open pit and underground. FY2026 output 328,600 oz Au and 14,594 t Cu, beating the top of upgraded guidance (260,000-310,000 oz). ~22Mt surface stockpiles give 12+ months of baseload mill feed.
- Havieron (gold-copper): high-grade underground, 45km east of Telfer. Resource 7.0Moz Au and 275kt Cu. Feasibility Study evaluating 4.0-4.5Mtpa (up from 2.8Mtpa). First ore processing at Telfer targeted for FY2028.
- O'Callaghans: tungsten-copper-zinc-lead skarn within 30km of the Telfer mill. Maiden resource 70Mt at 0.35% WO3, 0.30% Cu, 0.57% Zn, 0.28% Pb.
- Regional exploration: ~2,300 sq km Paterson tenure (Scallywag; Paterson South JV with Rio Tinto) plus Ernest Giles in the Yilgarn Craton.

## Financial position and funding
- Cash A$1,208m at 31 March 2026, rising to A$1,289m at 30 June 2026; plus an undrawn A$75m working capital facility.
- Debt-free (nil drawn debt).
- Binding commitment for a A$500m corporate debt facility with a Tier-1 syndicate (ANZ, HSBC, ING, NAB, Westpac): Facility A $250m 5-year RCF, Facility B $225m 7-year RCF, $25m contingent instrument facility.
- Q3 FY2026 operating cash flow A$453m; quarterly net cash build A$260m. Q3 AISC A$2,056/oz Au against realised gold prices above A$6,700/oz.

## Recent developments / catalysts
- ASX dual listing completed 24 June 2025 (ticker GGP) after a UK Scheme of Arrangement and 20:1 share consolidation.
- Federal EPBC environmental approval received for Havieron; WA state EPA approval is the remaining final regulatory step.
- FY2026 operational beat delivered (328,600 oz gold).

## Main risks
- Commodity price sensitivity (gold and copper); cushioned by put options - e.g. 219,706 oz under option at a blended strike of A$4,106/oz through CY26.
- Development execution: underground delays, water/ground management, raisebore construction, mill modification risk at Telfer.
- Regulatory: pending WA state EPA approval before full Havieron construction can advance.
- Native title and heritage obligations (Martu people partnerships).
