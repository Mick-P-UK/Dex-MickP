# Video Section Summary (Timestamped Bullets)

## Prompt

Please create a short bullet point summary (<130 words), with time stamps of the main sections, noting that the edited video is ....m .....sec long. No more than 7 sections please.

## Notes

- Purpose: turn an edited video into a short, skimmable bullet summary with timestamped section markers. Works well for YouTube chapters or a webinar recap.
- Fill the dots (....m .....sec) with the finished video's actual run time, e.g. "12m 34sec".
- The section cap (7) and the word cap (<130) keep the output tight. Adjust either number to suit a longer or shorter video.
