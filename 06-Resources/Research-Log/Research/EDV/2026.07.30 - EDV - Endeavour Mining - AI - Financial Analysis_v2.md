## Introduction

This report covers the Q2 and H1 2026 results released on 30 July 2026 (six months to 30 June 2026), together with a technical read of the 12-month price chart. The shares trade at approximately GBP 34.87 (3,487p) as at 30 July 2026 per the ShareScope chart, down about 2.81% on the results-day session despite a record set of numbers - a classic "sell the news" reaction after a strong run. Note the notebook's cached 4,401p quote is a stale April figure; the 30 July chart price is authoritative. The report scope covers the P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, management commentary, technical analysis and a rated recommendation.

## Detailed Analysis

### P&L Analysis
- **Turnover:** H1 2026 revenue rose 25.3% to $2,569.3m (H1 2025: $2,050.0m) [1]. The entire uplift and more came from price - the average realised gold price jumped to $4,579/oz from $3,107/oz - partially offset by lower sold volumes (production fell to 564koz from 647koz on grade sequencing and the Mana step-down).
- **Gross profit and margins:** Earnings from mine operations (gross profit) surged 36.1% to $1,379.4m on a 14.8% rise in cost of sales to $1,189.9m [1]. Cost inflation was concentrated in royalties, which climbed 58.8% to $243.5m as government take scales with the gold price (the Cote d'Ivoire royalty stepped from 6% to 8%). Depreciation actually fell 7.3% to $301.6m on lower produced ounces.
- **Operating profit:** After corporate costs ($25.9m), share-based comp ($23.0m) and exploration expensing ($22.8m), the operating result flowed through strongly. A net credit-loss reversal of +$5.1m (versus a $14.2m expense a year ago) helped.
- **PBT and net profit:** PBT grew 60.6% to $1,219.3m (H1 2025: $759.0m) [1]. The tax charge more than doubled to $462.8m in line with higher taxable earnings, leaving total net and comprehensive earnings of $756.5m (+33.9%) and net profit attributable to shareholders of $604.8m. Adjusted net earnings were $672.0m (+68.8%).
- **One-off items:** No impairments on mining interests in either period ($0.0m) [1]. The net loss on financial instruments narrowed sharply to $28.6m (H1 2025: $82.8m, which had carried a $133.1m hedge-programme loss now fully settled). H1 2026 also absorbed $14.1m of legal/regulatory supplier claims at Lafigue and Mana.
- **EPS:** Basic IFRS EPS of $2.50 (H1 2025: $1.83) and adjusted basic EPS of $2.78 (+69.5%) [1].

### Balance Sheet Analysis
- **Net cash/net debt:** A material balance-sheet transformation - EDV moved to a net cash position of $253.6m at 30 June 2026, from net debt of $157.5m at 31 December 2025 [1]. Note net cash was actually higher at $405.4m at 31 March; Q2 absorbed the H2-2025 dividend, buybacks and seasonal tax upstreaming.
- **Liquidity and gearing:** Total cash of $1,251.8m against gross debt of $998.2m ($500m Senior Notes, $410m drawn RCF with $290m undrawn, $88.2m Lafigue term loan) [1]. Leverage sits at just 0.09x net cash to LTM adjusted EBITDA, far inside the 0.50x policy ceiling.
- **Equity and share count:** Equity attributable to shareholders rose to $3,373.0m (from $3,034.8m). Ordinary shares in issue were 241.7m (241,655,222) at 30 June, with ~246.0m fully diluted [1]. The company repurchased 1.2m shares for $71.5m in H1.
- **Working capital:** H1 net working-capital outflow of $38.6m was a $103.5m improvement on H1 2025; Q2 alone delivered a $52.4m inflow on payables timing and receivables collection. No goodwill or mining-interest impairments.

### Cash Flow Analysis
- **Operating cash flow:** Up 41.4% to $1,054.9m ($4.36/sh) on higher price-driven revenue and lighter working-capital drag, partly offset by higher royalties and tax [1].
- **Capex and free cash flow:** Total mine capex of $263.4m ($150.0m sustaining, $98.8m non-sustaining, $14.6m growth), with total investing outflow of $293.5m. Free cash flow rose 48.2% to a record $761.4m ($3.15/sh) [1].
- **Cash balances:** Opened at $453.3m, closed at $1,251.8m - a net increase of $798.5m for the half (H1 2025: +$250.0m), flattered by financing inflows (RCF draw) of $61.1m [1].
- **Returns:** H1 shareholder returns of $301.5m (a record $230.0m / ~$0.95 per share interim dividend plus $71.5m of buybacks), comfortably more than double the annualised minimum commitment [1].

### Management Commentary and Audit Notes
- **Outlook and H2 weighting:** CEO Ian Cockerill flagged a solid H1 and reiterated full-year guidance of 1,090-1,265 koz at AISC $1,600-1,800/oz (royalty-adjusted at a $3,000/oz base). Both production and costs are H2-weighted: Q3 sees lower throughput and grades on the wet season and phased waste stripping, before a significant Q4 lift [1].
- **Growth pipeline (catalysts):** Assafou (Tanda-Iguela, Cote d'Ivoire) is the flagship - $5.1bn after-tax NPV5 and 55% IRR at $4,000/oz gold, FEED complete, Mining Convention targeted for late Q3 and a Final Investment Decision by year-end 2026. Sabodala-Massawa underground first ore and the BIOX optimisation are due by year-end, and maiden/updated resources at Vindaloo Deeps and Kawsara land in H2. Lafigue continues to run above nameplate.
- **Site watch:** Mana is tracking below the low end of guidance (Siou depletion, Aviera pause); Lafigue is in the top half and Hounde, Ity and Sabodala-Massawa are on track [1].
- **Risks:** West African security (terrorism, kidnapping, civil unrest), unpredictable fiscal/royalty regimes across five jurisdictions, artisanal-mining encroachment, and safety - there were two contractor fatalities in 2026 (Mana, 6 March; Lafigue, 29 May), prompting an external safety audit across all sites [1].
- **Going concern and audit:** The Board assessed going concern through at least August 2027 and confirmed it remains appropriate. The H1 statements are unaudited (standard for interims); the last audited FY2025 accounts carried an unqualified BDO LLP opinion with no emphasis-of-matter [1].

## Overall Summary and Recommendation

Concrete levels first (these are stable between runs): technical stop/watch level ~GBP 35 (a clean break targets GBP 29-30); upside pivot ~GBP 40 (reclaiming the 200-day), then GBP 44-45; broker consensus is Buy with price targets of GBX 5,200-6,000 (Berenberg 5,200, JPMorgan 5,900, RBC 6,000) [2]; chart captured 2026.07.30 at ~GBP 34.87.

Fundamentally this is one of the strongest half-year prints in the sector: revenue +25%, PBT +61%, record free cash flow of $761m, a swing to $253.6m net cash, and record shareholder returns of $301.5m, all with full-year guidance reaffirmed and a value-accretive FID (Assafou) due by year-end. The valuation math is compelling versus my reconciled ~$5.05 basic EPS and ~4% dividend yield - and the market's ShareScope consensus, while overstated on revenue and free cash flow (its EPS and dividend lines actually reconcile with my estimates), at least confirms a rich dividend. The tension is purely technical: the shares are mid-correction, below the 50- and 200-day, on a results-day "sell the news."

On balance this is a **BUY** for investors able to accumulate into weakness, best framed as scaling in around the GBP 35 support shelf and GBP 29-30 rather than chasing, with the year-end Assafou FID and Q4 production ramp as the re-rating catalysts. Shorter-term traders may prefer to wait for a reclaim of GBP 40 (200-day) to confirm the downtrend has ended - so a defensible **HOLD** stance is reasonable until that pivot. This is a high-quality operational story on sale, not a special-situation or deep-value distressed call. Treat the single rating word as secondary to the levels above, since it can shift with the intraday chart.
