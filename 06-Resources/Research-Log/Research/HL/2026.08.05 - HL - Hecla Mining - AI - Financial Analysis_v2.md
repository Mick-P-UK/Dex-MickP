## Introduction

Hecla Mining Company is North America's largest primary silver producer, operating three continuing mines: Greens Creek (Alaska), Lucky Friday (Idaho) and Keno Hill (Yukon, Canada), producing silver plus by-product gold, zinc and lead. Following the sale of its Casa Berardi gold mine to Orezone Gold on 25 March 2026, Hecla is now a near-pure-play silver vehicle, and Casa Berardi is reported as a discontinued operation with all prior periods recast [1]. This report analyses the freshest financials - the SEC Form 10-Q for Q2 2026 and the earnings release dated 4 August 2026 (yesterday) - alongside the FY2025 10-K and ShareScope consensus. Headline figures are stated on a CONTINUING OPERATIONS basis, which is the SEC-filed authority; where ShareScope consensus still blends Casa Berardi, the SEC continuing-operations figures take precedence and this is flagged throughout. All figures are in USD. The shares trade at approximately $15.41 (from the ShareScope chart, 5 August 2026), down roughly 55% from the January/February 2026 blow-off high near $34, but multiples of the mid-2025 base around $6-7.

## Detailed Analysis

### P&L Analysis

- **Turnover:** Continuing-operations sales rose to $745.3m in H1 2026 from $424.3m in H1 2025 (+75.6%), and to $333.9m in Q2 2026 from $219.0m in Q2 2025 (+52.5%) [1]. The driver is price, not volume: H1 silver production of 8.11 Moz was actually slightly DOWN year-on-year (from 8.62 Moz), while realised silver leapt to $73.14/oz for H1 (Q2 $63.06/oz) from ~$34/oz a year earlier - an ~81% Q2 uplift [1]. Realised gold of $4,256/oz (Q2) is similarly elevated.
- **Gross profit and margins:** H1 gross profit (after DD&A) was $433.1m on a segment basis versus $154.0m a year earlier; the H1 pre-tax margin was 47.3% [1]. Costs applicable to sales rose only modestly ($241.7m H1 vs $209.2m), so almost all incremental revenue dropped through - textbook operating leverage on a fixed-cost mine base against a silver price spike.
- **Operating profit:** Operating income was $368.8m for H1 (H1 2025: $105.4m) and $145.7m for Q2 (Q2 2025: $58.0m) [1].
- **Pre-tax and net profit (one-offs called out):** Income from continuing operations before tax was $352.2m H1 / $136.6m Q2. Income from continuing operations after tax was $282.5m H1 / $117.9m Q2 [1]. Several one-offs distort the picture: (i) a $192.5m non-cash loss on the Casa Berardi disposal and an $11.5m closure-plan set-off liability, both inside discontinued operations, dragging TOTAL H1 net income down to $98.8m; (ii) a $0.9m loss on the Senior Notes redemption; (iii) a $2.4m loss on exiting immaterial Mexican subsidiaries; and (iv) a favourable Klondex tax reclassification (release of a historical valuation allowance) that cut the effective tax rate to 13.7% in Q2 and 19.8% in H1 [1]. Silver put options (a $25m premium paid in December 2025 at a $50/oz strike) produced a $7.6m unrealised H1 mark-to-market loss.
- **EPS implications:** Diluted EPS from CONTINUING operations was $0.17 for Q2 (Q2 2025: $0.04) and $0.42 for H1 (H1 2025: $0.08) [1]. The 4 August release headlined continuing-ops income of ~$118m at approximately $0.18/share (basic); the precise SEC-filed GAAP diluted figure is $0.17 ($117.876m / 675.886m diluted shares), the small difference being basic-vs-diluted/rounding - I anchor on the $0.17 GAAP diluted figure. TOTAL diluted EPS (after the discontinued-ops loss) was just $0.15 for H1, which materially understates underlying earning power.

### Balance Sheet Analysis

- **Net cash / debt:** Hecla ended Q2 2026 DEBT-FREE excluding finance leases. On 9 April 2026 it fully redeemed the remaining $263m of 7.25% Senior Notes due 2028 (total payment $265.8m including $2.8m accrued interest), following an August 2025 partial redemption of $212m [1]. Cash and equivalents stood at $483.5m against $0 borrowings, i.e. net cash of $483.5m (or $470.7m including $12.7m of finance leases). CEO Rob Krcmarov describes this as the strongest balance sheet in Hecla's history [1].
- **Share count:** 671,734,340 shares outstanding at 30 June 2026 (680,925,226 issued less 9,190,886 treasury); 671,768,431 at 31 July 2026. Weighted-average diluted shares were 675.886m (Q2) and 675.865m (H1) [1].
- **Liquidity:** Total current assets $772.0m; total assets $3,185.3m; total liabilities $507.5m; stockholders' equity $2,677.8m [1]. The $225m revolving credit facility (plus a $75m accordion, maturing July 2028) was undrawn, with $221.5m available after $3.5m of letters of credit.
- **Other material items:** Accrued reclamation and closure liabilities fell to $127.6m (from $202.3m at end-2025) after the Casa Berardi exit, supported by $221.7m of surety bonds. There is no material pension deficit (a SERP Rabbi-Trust reclassification was a presentation correction, not an economic hit) [1].

### Cash Flow Analysis

- **Operating cash flow:** Continuing-operations operating cash flow was a very strong $357.8m for H1 (H1 2025: $136.0m) and $174.9m for Q2 [1]. Consolidated (including the Casa Berardi stub) operating cash flow was $369.2m.
- **Capex and free cash flow:** Capital additions were $78.4m H1 / $39.1m Q2, essentially flat year-on-year, yielding continuing-operations free cash flow of $279.4m H1 (H1 2025: $55.5m) and $135.8m Q2 [1]. Adjusted EBITDA was $464.3m H1 / $199.2m Q2 - an H1 EBITDA margin of ~62%.
- **Opening/closing cash:** Total cash (including restricted) opened FY2026 at $242.7m and closed H1 at $484.7m [1]. Within Q2, cash fell sequentially from $588.7m (1 April) to $484.7m (30 June), entirely because the ~$263m Senior Notes redemption absorbed the Casa Berardi sale proceeds - a deliberate de-leveraging, not operational cash burn.
- **Net cash movement:** H1 investing was a +$166.7m inflow (boosted by $178.2m net proceeds from Casa Berardi and $5.2m from the Mexican disposals); financing was a -$276.5m outflow (the notes redemption) [1].

### Management Commentary and Audit Notes

- **Post-period / operational update (4 August 2026 release):** Lucky Friday set a QUARTERLY silver production record of 1.53 Moz on a 31% higher milled grade (management flags this grade is not sustainable long-term) [1]. Consolidated Q2 silver production was 4.21 Moz (up 8% sequentially). Full-year silver guidance was tightened to 15.1-16.1 Moz and cost guidance was LOWERED (consolidated cash cost of -$4.00 to -$3.75/oz after by-product credits; AISC $12.50-$13.50/oz) [1]. Keno Hill silver guidance was cut to 2.2-2.6 Moz to prioritise permitting and infrastructure.
- **Growth pipeline:** Greens Creek Phase 2W dry-stack tailings extension was approved post-quarter (Phase 2E fills in October 2026); a pyrite-circuit project could add 1.0-1.2 Moz silver and 10-15 koz gold annually from Q4 2027-H1 2028; $16m is allocated to evaluate a Nevada Midas mill restart [1].
- **Material risks:** Keno Hill remains the key operational overhang - Yukon Energy (Aishihik hydro) power curtailments cut ~130koz of silver and added $0.5m of idle labour cost across late 2025/early 2026 (16 days out in December 2025, 5 days in January 2026), and permitting/waste-rock limits create genuine 2027-2029 continuity uncertainty [1]. Lucky Friday carries deep-mining seismic risk and a disruption history (2017-2020 strike; 2023 shaft fire). Casa Berardi tail risks remain: an Orezone set-off right against deferred payments (closure plan approved at CAD 237.1m, above the CAD/USD 150m threshold, forcing an $11.5m accrual) and a Hecla reimbursement obligation for HM3 impoundment penalties (Quebec prosecutors seek $2.7m) [1]. Legacy CERCLA/Superfund exposures (San Mateo Creek, Carpenter Snow Creek >$100m estimated) persist.
- **Going concern / audit:** No going-concern issue - management confirms liquidity is sufficient. Auditor BDO USA issued an UNQUALIFIED opinion on FY2025 with NO emphasis-of-matter; the sole Critical Audit Matter was concentrate-sales revenue recognition. Prior periods were recast (not restated for error) for the Casa Berardi discontinued-operations presentation, plus a minor out-of-period SERP reclassification [1].
