## Introduction

Hochschild Mining PLC is a precious-metals producer (gold and silver) operating principally in the Americas - its core assets are the Inmaculada and San Jose mines in Peru/Argentina and the Mara Rosa mine in Brazil. The company is London-listed but reports its financial statements in US dollars, so every monetary figure in this report is stated in USD millions unless flagged otherwise, while the share price is quoted in UK pence (GBX). At the chart capture the shares traded at 453.5p, up 3.02% on the day (ShareScope, 4 Aug 2026). This report covers the audited FY2025 results (year ended 31 December 2025, released as Preliminary Results on 11 March 2026), folds in every LSE RNS since that date - most importantly the 22 July 2026 H1 2026 production and cash update - and sets out a full-year FY2026 forecast with a technical read of the 12-month chart.

**Currency and units note (critical for this stock):** ShareScope's per-share fields are inconsistently labelled. The income-statement CSV tags EPS with the suffix "(p)", implying pence, but the figures are mathematically US cents - cross-checking FY2025 attributable profit of USD 201.9m divided by 514.5m shares gives 39.2 US cents, matching the "EPS reported (p)" row value of 39.0 [C22][1]. The forecasts CSV correctly labels the same series "EPS (c)". Throughout this report EPS is therefore stated in US cents. Dividends are genuinely in pence (Hochschild declares in US cents then converts to sterling for payment - the dividends CSV shows the final FY2025 dividend as 3.69p). Where I convert USD to GBP I use an assumed rate of GBP/USD = 1.35 (an assumption for August 2026, flagged at each use).

## Detailed Analysis

### P&L Analysis

- **Turnover:** FY2025 revenue was USD 1,182.1m, up 24.7% on FY2024's USD 947.7m [C3], which itself was up 36.6% on FY2023's USD 693.7m [C4] (Income Statement CSV). The two-year surge reflects the Mara Rosa mine ramp-up and a sharply rising gold price. Consensus sees a further +40.4% to USD 1,660.0m in FY2026 [C7] on the current metal-price environment (Forecasts CSV).
- **Gross profit and margin:** FY2025 gross profit was USD 504.2m, a gross margin of 42.7% [C1] - the strongest in over a decade and up from 36.1% in FY2024 and 26.7% in FY2023 (Ratios CSV confirms 42.7%). Operating leverage on rising prices is clearly visible.
- **Operating profit:** Standardised operating profit was USD 345.4m in FY2025 (operating margin 29.2% [C2]) versus USD 226.6m in FY2024 and just USD 83.8m in FY2023.
- **PBT and net profit (watch the one-offs):** Reported FY2025 PBT was USD 372.8m, but this included a positive one-off of roughly USD 36.5m - normalised PBT was USD 336.3m [1]. The prior years ran the other way: FY2023 reported PBT of -USD 43.5m contained a circa USD 91m exceptional charge (normalised PBT was +USD 47.6m), and FY2024 reported PBT of USD 177.2m was USD 16.2m below normalised [1]. FY2025 net profit attributable was USD 201.9m (post-tax USD 247.4m before USD 45.5m minorities). The FY2025 tax charge was USD 125.4m (effective rate 33.6% [C5], Ratios CSV).
- **EPS:** FY2025 reported EPS was 39.0 US cents and normalised continuous EPS was 34.3 US cents (Income Statement CSV, "(p)"-labelled but cents per [C22][1]), versus 19.0 cents reported in FY2024. Consensus forecasts FY2026 normalised EPS of 70.6 US cents (+127.8%, Ratios/Forecasts CSVs), reflecting the revenue and margin step-up.

### Balance Sheet Analysis

- **Net debt/cash position:** ShareScope's Balance Sheet CSV shows FY2025 net borrowing of USD 31.7m (net debt); Hochschild's own FY2025 figure was net debt of USD 22.7m (RNS, 11 Mar 2026) - a small presentational difference (ShareScope includes leases). The transformative development is post-period: at 30 June 2026 the group held cash and equivalents of USD 309m and swung to NET CASH of USD 51m, a +USD 73.7m improvement in six months [C14] (RNS, 22 Jul 2026). Consensus projects net cash of USD 199.7m by 31 December 2026 (Forecasts CSV).
- **Share count:** 514.5m shares in issue at FY2025, with average diluted shares of 514.5m (Income Statement CSV) - the count has been broadly stable, so EPS growth is earnings-driven, not dilution-distorted.
- **NAV:** FY2025 shareholders' funds were USD 721.6m; ShareScope shows NAV per share of 110.0p (its stated pence basis). The FY2026 forecast NAV is USD 1,248.2m, which is 242.6 US cents per share (about 179.7p at GBP/USD 1.35) as retained earnings build [C26] (Balance Sheet/Forecasts CSVs).
- **Pension and other items:** No material pension liabilities are recorded (Balance Sheet CSV pension rows are blank). Working capital was roughly neutral at FY2025 (-USD 3.8m), a normalisation after prior-year swings.

### Cash Flow Analysis

- **Operating cash flow:** FY2025 gross operating cash flow was USD 473.9m (up from USD 365.0m in FY2024); net cash from operations after tax and interest was USD 423.9m (Cash Flow CSV). ShareScope does not publish an FY2026 operating-cash-flow forecast; applying the FY2025 operating-cash-flow-to-EBITDA ratio of 0.83 to the FY2026 EBITDA forecast of USD 922.8m implies roughly USD 766m [C27] - my estimate.
- **Capex and FCF:** FY2025 capex was USD 218.1m (Cash Flow CSV), giving FY2025 free cash flow (equity) of USD 198.0m - a strong recovery from -USD 86.3m in FY2023. Consensus FY2026 capex is USD 293.9m with FCF of USD 283.1m (Forecasts CSV). The H1 2026 net-cash swing corroborates the FCF trajectory.
- **Cash balances:** FY2025 closed at USD 109.8m cash on the ShareScope balance-sheet line; the 30 June 2026 RNS reports USD 309m cash, reflecting strong H1 receipts at elevated prices.
- **Net cash movement:** H1 2026 saw USD 84m of dividends paid (USD 26m to shareholders, USD 58m to the San Jose JV partner McEwen Mining) [C15] yet the group still built net cash - underlining cash-generative operations at current metal prices (RNS, 22 Jul 2026).

### Management Commentary and Audit Notes

- **FY2025 results (11 Mar 2026):** Record revenue and margins, a return to positive reported PBT, a rebuilt balance sheet and a rising dividend. Going concern was unqualified; the low net-debt position at year-end has since turned to net cash.
- **Risks identified:** Cost inflation (AISC pressure), Argentine macro/currency exposure at San Jose, the 51%-owned San Jose JV structure (minority leakage - see below), and sensitivity to gold/silver prices, which cut both ways.
- **JV nuance (important):** The 22 July 2026 mine breakdown quotes San Jose at 58,800 GEO on a 100% basis, but Hochschild owns only 51%, so the group's ATTRIBUTABLE total (151,830 GEO) is below the consolidated sum of the three mines (180,644 GEO [C12]). P&L revenue is reported on the consolidated basis with the minority stripped out below the line, which is why consensus revenue of USD 1,660m reconciles with consolidated H1 output of roughly USD 752.7m [C13] rather than with the lower attributable figure.
- **Governance / audit note:** A change of auditor is in progress - the 8 April 2026 RNS "Further re Change of Auditor" followed the initial notice. A change of external auditor is a governance item worth monitoring (transition risk, restatement risk), though nothing in the FY2025 accounts suggests an adverse driver.

The two cautions are AISC running 5-10% above guidance (which is why my forecasts sit modestly below consensus) and a share price still working off a parabolic February top. This is not a deep-value or special-situation call - it is a cash-generative, cheaply-rated gold/silver producer mid-correction.

**Recommendation: BUY** (treat the single word as secondary to the levels above - it can shift with intraday chart movement and normal model variance between runs).
