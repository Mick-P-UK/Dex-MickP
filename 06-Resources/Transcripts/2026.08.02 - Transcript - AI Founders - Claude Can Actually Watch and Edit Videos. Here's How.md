## Summary

A walkthrough of a four-step AI pipeline for fixing B-roll in YouTube videos, run
through Claude (here called Fable 5). Step 1 "Watch": an editor drops a Frame.io link in
Slack, which triggers an automation; Claude pulls the auto-generated, frame-timestamped
transcript through a custom Frame.io MCP and reads it line by line against the visuals
(it "sees but cannot hear", so the transcript is the source of truth). Step 2 "Marks":
guided by a client-specific Claude skill that encodes the channel's taste, it flags every
moment where B-roll is missing or emotionally mismatched and posts frame-accurate briefs
back to Frame.io. Step 3 "Render": Claude drives Higgsfield through its MCP to generate
each clip, auto-routing between models (SeeDance, GPT Image, Soul) and naming files by
timecode. Step 4 "Drop back": it uploads each clip onto the matching Frame.io comment so
the editor just drags it into the cut. The human keeps the final cut, and the presenter
frames the whole loop as a sellable "senior producer review" service on a retainer.

## Key Takeaways

- Four-step loop: Watch (read the frame-timestamped transcript) -> Marks (flag
  missing/mismatched B-roll with specific briefs) -> Render (generate via Higgsfield MCP)
  -> Drop back (attach clips to the Frame.io comments).
- Claude "sees but cannot hear": it reads Frame.io's audio transcript, not the audio -
  the transcript is the source of truth for every timecode.
- A client-specific Claude skill encodes "taste" (which shots, emotions and lengths fit
  the channel), turning a generic "needs B-roll" flag into a producer-grade brief.
- Two custom MCPs do the work: a Frame.io MCP (pull transcript, read/post comments) and
  the Higgsfield MCP (generate clips, auto-route models). The Claude Chrome extension is
  the lower-tech fallback.
- Timecodes are captured by clicking words in the transcript so Frame.io jumps the
  playhead to the exact frame.
- The human stays in the loop for the final cut (pacing, length) - deliberately not full
  automation.
- Productised as a service: sell "senior producer review", not B-roll, on a monthly
  retainer per creator.
- Relevance to us: the pattern of transcript-as-source-of-truth + a task-specific skill
  that encodes taste + an MCP to an external tool mirrors how we build skills.
