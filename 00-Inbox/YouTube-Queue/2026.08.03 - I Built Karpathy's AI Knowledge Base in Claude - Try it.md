SUMMARY

Simon builds a Karpathy-style AI knowledge base from scratch inside Claude
Cowork, deliberately without Obsidian and without code. The stated
architecture is three folders plus a single CLAUDE.md, assembled through a
five-step framework, and finished with a Claude Skill that runs a monthly
system health check as a scheduled task. The video runs from setup, through
a bulk information dump, into building the wiki layer, then adds a
self-improvement loop and the automated health check before showing the
final result. It is a build-along, and it is also a funnel for his paid
Cowork OS product, so the free artefacts are the CLAUDE.md template, the
build prompt and the Health Check skill.

ANGLES FOR MCSB

1. Same source pattern, different tooling. This is the Karpathy LLM Wiki
pattern I already wanted folded into MCSB, so it is a second independent
read on the same idea. Worth comparing his three folders against my
raw-data-plus-wiki split, and seeing whether his third folder is doing
something my two do not.

2. Chapter 20:37 - the compounding loop. This is the piece that matters
most, given memory is the next thing on my list. Need to know what actually
triggers the update, what it writes, and where it writes it.

3. Chapter 24:22 - health check as a scheduled skill. A recurring integrity
check is a direct answer to the mirror-drift problem. Even if his
implementation is Cowork-specific, a scheduled audit skill over the vault
and the skills registry is transferable.

4. Chapter 11:20 - the information dump. How he gets bulk material in
without curating it first. Relevant to the single-inbox rule.
